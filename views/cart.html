Shipping & returns

Orders ship from our Bend, Oregon warehouse within two business days. Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Expedited options are calculated once your order is confirmed.

Unused gear can be returned within 60 days. Tents and sleeping bags must be returned dry and packed in their stuff sacks. Return shipping is free for exchanges.

Oversized items such as tents and stoves may arrive in a separate box.

Frequently asked questions

Do you ship internationally?
Not yet. We currently ship to the 48 contiguous US states.

Can I change my order?
Email us within an hour of ordering and we will do our best.

Is my tent seam-sealed?
Every Ridgeline tent is factory seam-taped and tested in our rain room.

Do you offer repairs?
Yes. Our repair bench fixes zippers, poles and tears at cost.

Where are your products made?
Our down is responsibly sourced and our packs are sewn in small batches.

Join the Ember & Oak trail club

Members get early access to new gear, trip guides and members-only meetups. It is free to join.
